# Recorded sounds of people

Three sounds that synthesis could not make believable. All are free of any credit requirement (public domain or CC0). Each file holds a few takes cut from one source recording, with 0.3 s of silence between them, mono, peak at -1 dBFS; `src/game/recordings.ts` lists where each take starts and how long it lasts, and plays one at random.

| File | Source | Licence | Takes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `nysning.mp3` | [Sneezing.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sneezing.ogg), Wikimedia Commons | Public domain | Two sneezes |
| `suck.mp3` | [Three sighs.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Three_sighs.ogg), Wikimedia Commons | Public domain | Three sighs |
| `klirr.mp3` | [Bottles Clink](https://freesound.org/people/CHallSmith/sounds/870721/) by CHallSmith, Freesound | CC0 | Three clinks of bottles being picked up |

To change a take, cut it again from the source and update the list in `recordings.ts`. Do not add other audio here.
